# Outcome

What should a user or system be able to do after this change?

## Current evidence

- Exact failing behavior and a reproduction.
- Relevant files, logs or public references; omit credentials and private data.
- What has already been checked and what remains uncertain.

## Acceptance

- Observable behavior, including a realistic failure or boundary case.
- Exact local checks already used by this repository.
- Whether merge is sufficient, or deployment/live acceptance is still needed.

## Scope and decisions

- Included changes and explicit exclusions.
- Related issues/PRs and their owners.
- Product or architecture choices that need an owner decision.
- Actions authorized now; actions that need a separate decision.
